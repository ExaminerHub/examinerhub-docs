# 1. Executive Summary

ExaminerHub is a web-based platform designed specifically for Cambridge English examiners, supervisors, Team Leaders, centre managers and examination administrators.

It replaces fragmented emails, spreadsheets and manual invoicing with a single secure application that manages the complete examiner lifecycle.

The application allows examiners to:

* Receive and accept sessions
* View weekly and daily schedules
* Access all session information from one place
* Record speaking and invigilation work
* Track monitoring and certification
* Generate invoices automatically
* Monitor earnings
* Communicate with examination centres
* Maintain a complete professional record

The objective of ExaminerHub is to reduce administration, improve communication and provide a modern digital workspace for everyone involved in Cambridge English examinations.

---

# 2. Product Vision

To become the leading operational platform for Cambridge English examination centres by providing one trusted place where examiners can manage every aspect of their professional work.

---

# 3. Mission Statement

Less administration. More examining.

---

# 4. Problem Statement

Current processes rely heavily on:

* Email
* PDF timetables
* Manual notes
* Spreadsheets
* Manual invoice preparation

Important information is often difficult to locate after the session has finished.

Examiners frequently need to search through months of emails to remember candidate numbers, partner examiners, session details and travel information when preparing invoices.

ExaminerHub eliminates this problem by storing all session information permanently in one secure location.

---

# 5. Primary Goal

Every examiner should be able to complete an entire quarter's invoicing in less than 30 seconds because the application already knows everything about every session.
